ROMEO AIRLINES

Connecting People with the World

Founded in 2029 by Mohamed Elmustafa Ali Hussein, Romeo Airlines was established with a clear vision: to build a modern airline that connects people, cities, and communities with the world.

Romeo Airlines was created to provide reliable, efficient, and professional air transportation, offering convenient connections between major cities and regional destinations while providing passengers with a comfortable and dependable travel experience.

Headquartered in Port Sudan, with its main corporate office in Khartoum, Romeo Airlines will operate a growing network designed to connect passengers and businesses with key domestic, regional, and international destinations.

Our primary operational base will be Port Sudan New International Airport, serving as the foundation of our domestic and international network.

Romeo Airlines will initially focus on connecting major cities with key regional and international destinations, providing convenient and reliable air travel for passengers, businesses, families, and communities.

Our fleet strategy will include a combination of Boeing 737s, Fokker 50s, Fokker 70s, Dash 8 aircraft, Bombardier Q Series aircraft, and ATR 72-500s, allowing Romeo Airlines to efficiently serve both major airports and regional destinations.

The airline will commence full commercial operations in 2030, In Shaa Allah, beginning a new chapter in modern aviation.

Our Future

Romeo Airlines is being built for the long term.

We believe aviation should bring people closer together, create opportunities, and make travel more accessible, efficient, and enjoyable.
