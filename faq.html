Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions.

How can I change my booking?

Use Manage Booking with your PNR and last name. Availability depends on your fare rules.

How do I check in online?

Use the Check-in page with your booking reference and surname.

How do I request a refund?

Contact Romeo Airlines or use the Manage Booking service when the production system is connected.

Where can I find my PNR?

Your PNR is the booking reference on your confirmation and e-ticket.
