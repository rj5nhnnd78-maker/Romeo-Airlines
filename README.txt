ROMEO AIRLINES — MANAGE BOOKING FLOW

The top navigation no longer contains Book a Flight, Check-in, or Flight Status.
These three are treated as journey/booking services and are accessed through Manage Booking.

Flow:
1. Book a Flight (complete booking workflow)
2. Automatic redirect to Manage Booking after PNR creation
3. Continue to Check-in
4. Automatic redirect to Flight Status after check-in

The three service cards are also grouped under Manage Booking.

CALENDAR UPDATE: Departure and Return fields now open a visual calendar with month/year selectors. Past dates are disabled.
